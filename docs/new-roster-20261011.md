Original geometry, UVs and eight bone influences preserved. 3,413 Brax vertices require more than four weights. Nyxa model/material identity retained; imports matching supplied clips, right block, paired shoulder throw, fall, flight and get-up. Brax ZIP's Idle and Medium Step Forward belong to Nyxa. Old Brax Kip Up and incompatible Walking excluded.

Brax uses the settled segment of supplied Center Block for a 2.2-second seamless moving guard. Full Warming Up is the entrance. Locomotion uses Long Step Forward, Step Backward, Running and Jump. Six attack source choices: Jab Cross, Hook, Boxing, Mma Kick, Kicking and Kicking (2). Brax combo joins jab/cross, hook and high kick (four damage groups); Nyxa retains seven-contact punch/elbow/kick combo. Contacts regenerated from actual animated bone positions at 60 samples/second, with separate startup/recovery speed. Additional source clips remain available but are not all assigned to controls.

Ordinary knockdown occurs after 28 unblocked impact points (counter multiplier 1.5). A dedicated airborne launch occurs after 50 unblocked damage points since the last launch, on an unblocked combo finisher, or on an unblocked hit at the HP floor. Normal damage and throws cannot reduce these two fighters below 1 HP. First launch recovers; second launch sets HP to zero only after the landing animation completes. Grounded falls and throws do not count. Training continues and resets the launch count after recovery. Round timer and other fighters' normal rules remain. 450 ms get-up protection.

Validation: source-motion audit (continuous guards and bone-centred contacts), actual eight-influence CPU mesh sampling (finite bounded skinning; weight error < 4.5e-8), exact original Brax texture hashes, contact-timing audit, new-fighter control/contact/miss audit in both directions, two-launch/recovery/paired-throw audit for both actors, fighter-polish timing audit.
